Help and support

Stuck, found a problem or have an idea? Here's the quickest way to reach us.

1. In the app: Feedback

Tap Help at the top of any screen, then Send feedback. Choose Something's wrong, An idea or A question, write what happened and tap Send. It reaches the OrdenZ team straight away, with the screen you were on, the app version and the kind of device, so you don't have to explain those.

Tick OrdenZ can email me about this if you'd like a reply. We answer at the cafe owner's email address.

2. On WhatsApp

{{#if waLink}}

Chat with us on WhatsApp

Send photos, screenshots or voice notes. In the app, Help → Chat with us on WhatsApp also adds your cafe, the screen you're on and the app version to the message, so we can help faster.

{{/if}}

3. By email

{{#if email}}{{email}}{{/if}}. Please include your cafe code (shown under Menu & settings), what you were doing, and a screenshot if you can. Never send passwords, PINs or card numbers.

When we reply

We aim to reply within one working day, India and Malaysia time. Messages marked URGENT in the subject (you can't take bills or payments) are looked at first.

If the internet or OrdenZ is down

  • Keep billing at the counter: bills are saved on the tablet with an offline number and sent automatically, never twice, when the connection is back.
  • Receipts still print on Bluetooth and network printers.
  • UPI at the kiosk and table QR ordering need internet. Take those orders at the counter for now.

Common questions

How do I set up my cafe?

Sign in as the owner and follow Get your cafe ready on the Owner screen: your menu, the details on your bills, payments, your team, a printer and a kiosk. The guide shows each step with pictures.

Can I practise before opening?

Yes. Ask us to turn on practice mode for your cafe: sample customers order every few minutes for a week, so your team can try the kiosk, kitchen and token screen. Practice bills are removed when you start selling for real.

A kiosk or token TV won't sign in

On the tablet, choose Kiosk / TV, enter your cafe code and the device key from Menu & settings. If the owner changed the device key or locked the device, pair it again.

I forgot my owner password

Email us from the owner's email address with your cafe code and we'll help you reset it.
