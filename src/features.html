Customers order themselves

The Kiosk and Token screen tabs in the app.

Self-order kiosk

Customers order themselves

  • Photo menu in English, தமிழ், हिन्दी, తెలుగు, മലയാളം or ಕನ್ನಡ: choose up to six for your kiosk
  • Add item photos, or set up menu items, options and recipes from Excel

Your customers' language

Eight languages on the kiosk

  • English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese
  • Pick up to six for your kiosk; customers tap to switch
  • Type your menu names, or let AI fill them in (Pro)

Voice & AI ordering

Just say the order

  • "Two large cappuccinos with oat milk and a brownie"
  • Understands English, Tamil and Tanglish, and with AI also Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada
  • The customer checks it and taps Add, so nothing goes wrong
Table QR ordering

Guests order from their own phone

  • Starter and Pro. A QR code on each table opens your menu: no app, no sign-up
  • Your staff accept the order first, then it goes to the kitchen
  • After a captain or accepted check is open, extra phones add lines to that same unpaid bill
  • The guest sees their order's progress on their phone
  • The table's bill is paid at the counter. Guest online share-pay is not built yet
Pickup slots

They pick 4:15. You don't juggle the queue.

  • Starter and Pro. You set how long a slot is and how many orders it can take
  • Guests open a public link, choose items and a time, then collect at the counter
  • The kitchen sees the cap, so 4:15 does not turn into twenty bags at once
  • They pay when they collect. Online pickup payment is not available. WhatsApp only shares the link

At the counter

The Counter, Cash drawer and TableTurn tabs.

Counter POS & printing

Bills in seconds

  • Fast billing with discounts, cash, UPI and card, on a phone, tablet, desktop or a wide screen
  • A phone on its side, or a short landscape window, opens the cart as a drawer so the menu stays full-width. Favourites start hidden on phone and tablet (you can show them)
  • GST receipts with CGST and SGST in India; SST, service charge and 5-sen cash rounding in Malaysia
  • Paperless billing is optional and off unless you turn it on. Send bill on WhatsApp stays either way
  • Thermal receipts use the English name. Stock lists, supplier bills and the kitchen sheet open the browser print dialog — no thermal needed. A print queue holds jobs if the printer is busy
  • Compatible Bluetooth and network thermal printers (58 mm and 80 mm); test your printer and cash-drawer setup before launch
UPI payments

The exact rupees. The QR does the rest.

  • A UPI QR for the exact amount on every kiosk order (Razorpay or PhonePe)
  • Payment is confirmed automatically; no one checks a phone
  • Wrong or late payments show up on Owner with a one-tap refund
  • Cash and card at the counter too. Merchant approval is arranged with the provider
DuitNow QR

The exact ringgit. Same idea, Malaysia.

  • Connect the cafe's approved Fiuu Offline Payment merchant for automatic DuitNow QR
  • The kiosk shows the exact MYR. Signed status confirms the payment; no one watches a phone
  • You can still record a received DuitNow at the counter if the customer paid another way
  • Refunds go through the Fiuu merchant portal. Live bank settlement needs provider approval
Split bills & open tabs

Pay it any way the table likes

  • Split one bill equally, by amount, or by the items each person had
  • Part UPI, part cash: the change is worked out for you
  • Captain: Pay later picks a floor table. Rounds append to that table's open check. The captain cannot collect cash, UPI or card
  • Cashier: Pay T4 settles the table. A typed name (Priya / takeaway) still works
  • Every payment is recorded against the bill, for clean reports
Cash drawer

Close the day with a counted drawer

  • Open a shift with the float in the drawer
  • Cash in and cash out, each with a reason
  • Count note by note at closing and see the difference straight away
  • Differences over your limit go to the owner or manager to review
  • A Z report for every shift
Offline billing

Internet down? Keep billing on Starter and Pro.

  • Starter and Pro: bills are saved on the tablet with an offline number
  • They sync automatically when you're back online, never twice
  • Receipts still print
  • Free needs a connection. UPI, DuitNow and table QR always need internet
Delivery apps

Swiggy at 7. Kitchen in a second. Payouts on Friday.

  • Turn each app on in House, with that app's commission and mark-up
  • Swiggy and Zomato in India; GrabFood, foodpanda and ShopeeFood in Malaysia — or another partner OrdenZ adds
  • Type the app's order at the counter, or take it from a signed webhook once that partner is onboarded (sandbox first)
  • Match the payouts from the app's settlement file on Reports, and see what's still unpaid
  • This is cafe intake and settlement, not a public Swiggy or Zomato partner listing by itself
TableTurn

Every table ready, every team member noticed

  • See every table at a glance: free, reserved, seated or needs cleaning
  • Bookings and a guest waitlist QR on Starter and Pro, with no double-booking and the right-size table suggested
  • A QR at the door: guests join and see the wait in minutes, no app
  • On Pro, guests leave and the clean-up job goes to the least busy person automatically
  • On Pro, a fresh photo of the clean table frees it for the next guests; the owner can send it back
  • On Pro, customers give staff a star from the QR code on their bill
  • Leaderboards, star of the month, and how fast your tables turn

Recipes & stock

Every plate the same, every rupee counted

Starting a new cafe

I haven't opened yet. Can OrdenZ help me get ready?

Yes. Set up free. 30 days of Pro from the first real bill or from day 15 if you have not billed yet. Then Free (1 paired device, 50 orders a day) unless you pick Starter or Pro. Practice bills do not start the trial. The Get your cafe ready checklist on Owner walks you through the menu, bill details, payments, team, printer and kiosk. Ask how to set this up under it talks you through the next step. Ask us for practice mode and sample customers will order every few minutes so your team can train before opening day.

Which licences do I need to open a cafe?

In India, usually FSSAI registration or a state licence, a shop and establishment registration, a trade licence from your local body, and GST once you cross the threshold. In Malaysia, SSM registration, a premises and signboard licence from your local council, food handler training and typhoid vaccination for staff, and SST once you cross the threshold. Our starter guide explains each one, with links to the official sites.

Do I need to charge GST or SST from day one?

Not always. In India a restaurant generally registers for GST once its turnover passes ₹20 lakh a year (₹10 lakh in some states). In Malaysia, food and beverage businesses register for service tax at RM1.5 million a year. Until then, set the tax to 0% (India) or leave the SST number empty (Malaysia), and OrdenZ prints bills without tax. Check your case with an accountant.

What equipment should I budget for?

Day one: Free is enough for one counter in a browser. A paired kiosk or token TV uses the one Free slot. Kiosk plus TV needs Starter (5 devices). A kitchen phone with a staff PIN is not a paired device. A TV does count. Add a Bluetooth or network thermal printer (58 or 80 mm) and a cash drawer that opens from the printer when you need them.

Using OrdenZ

What do I need to start?

A compatible Android tablet for the kiosk and a browser device for the counter or kitchen. A thermal printer and token display are optional. Test the exact hardware models during your pilot; hardware is quoted separately.

Can I try it before my first customer?

Yes. Try the live demo any time, or ask us to turn on practice mode for your own cafe: a sample customer orders about every 5 minutes for a week, so you can watch the kiosk, kitchen screen, token TV and reports working and train your team. Practice bills are marked and are removed when you tap Start selling for real, so your first real bill is bill 1.

Does it work without internet?

Offline counter billing is on Starter and Pro. Free needs a connection. Bills are saved on the tablet and sync automatically when the connection returns. UPI, DuitNow and table QR always need internet.

Which payments are supported?

In India, UPI through Razorpay or PhonePe with automatic confirmation, plus cash and card at the counter. In Malaysia, cash (rounded to 5 sen), card, and DuitNow QR through an approved Fiuu Offline Payment merchant, plus recording a received DuitNow at the counter. Live bank settlement needs provider approval. Discuss the payment provider before going live.

Does OrdenZ work in Malaysia?

Yes. Choose Malaysia when you sign up: prices are in RM, the kiosk speaks English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese (Tamil too if you like), and bills show your SSM number. Add your SST registration number to charge 6% SST, and set a service charge if you have one; SST is then charged on the service charge too. Not SST-registered? Leave SST at 0%. Connect Fiuu for automatic DuitNow QR, or record a received DuitNow at the counter. We're starting with a few pilot cafes in Malaysia: ask us to join.

How do the Pro trial and billing work?

Set up free. 30 days of Pro from the first real bill or from day 15 if you have not billed yet. Then Free (1 paired device, 50 orders a day) unless you pick Starter or Pro. Practice bills do not start the trial. Yearly is 12 months for the price of 10 (two months free). The number on the card is what you pay once for the year. Same for extras. India owners can use configured subscription checkout; Malaysia plans require a quote. You can also apply eligible promo codes or sign up with a referral code.

What is TableTurn?

TableTurn manages your tables and your team. Take bookings and keep a waiting list on Starter and Pro. Guests can scan a QR at the door to join and see the wait in minutes. On Pro, when guests leave a clean-up job goes to a staff member, a photo of the clean table makes it free for the next guests, and customers can give staff a star from the QR code on their bill.

Can staff clock in?

Yes. After PIN or email they land on Brief, which asks them to clock in — sign-in is not a clock. Skip to work is always there. Later snoozes a cook task for about an hour; Can't plus a reason lets the owner assign it again. Done does not cook a batch. Hours CSV and a Malaysia EPF/SOCSO or India PF/ESI working sheet are for the accountant. OrdenZ does not file with the government, and it is not a face or fingerprint terminal.

Can I send bills on WhatsApp instead of printing?

Yes, if you want. Paperless billing is optional and off until you turn it on in House. Send bill on WhatsApp still works when paperless is off. A paid kiosk thank-you shows a QR to view the English bill; unpaid cash-at-counter stays token only.

Does the till fit a phone or a tablet?

Yes. The counter fits a phone, tablet, desktop or a wide screen. A short landscape window (phone on its side, or a split window) opens the cart as a drawer. Favourites start hidden on phone and tablet; you can show them.

What extras can I add?

Starter and Pro stay the plans. Table QR is on Starter and Pro, not an extra. Paid extras — loyalty, campaigns, delivery pack, linked outlets, Malaysia e-invoice and the rest — start around ₹49 / RM4.90 a month, snack prices, not a fourth plan. Yearly is 12 months for the price of 10 (two months free). Request them from Plan.

What can I send my accountant?

From Reports → Accounting export: a sales day book, a purchase day book, a GST or SST working sheet (not a filed return), and Tally XML for the accountant to import. OrdenZ does not push live into Tally.

How do multiple outlets work?

On Pro plus the outlets extra, link the cafes you own (up to 50). Prove it once with that cafe's owner email, password and authenticator code if MFA is on. We do not keep that password (hash check only). If they change the password, link again. See a day or a date range, copy a menu, and send a stock transfer (draft → sent → received). Each outlet keeps its own stock. This is not a central warehouse.

Can I take bulk and catering orders?

Yes, on Starter and Pro. Record the customer, where the order came from, the event, who is responsible and the chef. OrdenZ shows what stock to buy, takes the ingredients from your normal stock when cooking starts, and tracks the advance and balance.

Can I manage my cafe from my laptop?

On Pro, yes: sign in from any web browser to manage the menu, stock, reports and settings. On Free and Starter, owner settings are in the OrdenZ app, and the counter, kitchen and stock screens work in any browser.

Which languages does it support?

The kiosk works in English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese; each cafe picks up to six. You can type your menu names in each language, or on Pro let AI fill them in for you to check. Voice input needs microphone permission and compatible device and service support.

Can I control what each staff member sees?

Yes. Owner plus 7 staff jobs including All-rounder, manager, captain, chef, cashier, store keeper and cleaning. Each staff job has its own PIN. Kiosk and token TV are not PIN roles. Managers can run the day but can't change your plan or billing. An activity log shows who changed what.

How does table service work?

The captain taps Pay later and picks a floor table. Rounds go on that table's open unpaid check. Extra phones scanning the table QR add lines to the same check. The cashier settles with Pay T4. The captain cannot collect cash, UPI or card.

What is Money Review?

On Owner, Money Review lists unusual money events for the owner or a manager — refunds, paid cancels, counter discounts, drawer events and Z variance. It is for review, not a theft verdict. Kitchen and captain cannot open it. The WhatsApp digest is a preview only.

Can guests book a pickup time?

Yes, on Starter and Pro. The owner sets the slot length and how many orders each slot can take. Guests pick items and a time on a public link, then pay when they collect. Online pickup payment is not available. WhatsApp only shares the link.

Can I keep track of supplier bills?

Yes, on Starter and Pro. Enter each supplier's bill with its items and GST, add a photo, and the stock and costs update by themselves. The store keeper can save a purchase draft from stock vs reorder; the owner or manager approves it. You see what you owe each supplier, what's overdue, the GST you paid this month, and your wastage.

You can enter menu items manually or use the Excel setup template. AI image import needs configuration and a review of names and prices. Ask us to assess your current export before switching; historical sales and supplier data are not automatically migrated.

Allow for compatible hardware, payment-provider fees and any AI or messaging usage. Discuss setup, migration, training and ongoing support before purchasing. Malaysia subscriptions are quote-based; the displayed pilot prices do not enable MYR checkout.

Who owns my data?

You do. We don't show ads or sell data, and you can delete your cafe account at any time. See our privacy policy.
