Starting a new cafe

I haven't opened yet. Can OrdenZ help me get ready?

Yes. Set up free. 30 days of Pro from the first real bill or from day 15 if you have not billed yet. Then Free (1 paired device, 50 orders a day) unless you pick Starter or Pro. Practice bills do not start the trial. The Get your cafe ready checklist on Owner walks you through the menu, bill details, payments, team, printer and kiosk. Ask how to set this up under it talks you through the next step. Ask us for practice mode and sample customers will order every few minutes so your team can train before opening day.

Which licences do I need to open a cafe?

Usually FSSAI registration or a state licence, a shop and establishment registration, a trade licence from your local body, and GST once you cross the threshold. Our starter guide explains each one, with links to the official sites.

Usually SSM registration, a premises and signboard licence from your local council, food handler training and typhoid vaccination for staff, and SST once you cross the threshold. Our starter guide explains each one, with links to the official sites.

Do I need to charge GST from day one?

Not always. A restaurant generally registers for GST once its turnover passes ₹20 lakh a year (₹10 lakh in some states). Until then, set the tax to 0%, and OrdenZ prints bills without tax. Check your case with an accountant.

Do I need to charge SST from day one?

Not always. Food and beverage businesses register for service tax at RM1.5 million a year. Until then, leave the SST number empty, and OrdenZ prints bills without tax. Check your case with an accountant.

What equipment should I budget for?

Day one: Free is enough for one counter in a browser. A paired kiosk or token TV uses the one Free slot. Kiosk plus TV needs Starter (5 devices). A kitchen phone with a staff PIN is not a paired device. A TV does count. Add a Bluetooth or network thermal printer (58 or 80 mm) and a cash drawer that opens from the printer when you need them.

A compatible Android tablet for the kiosk and a browser device for the counter or kitchen. A thermal printer and token display are optional. Test the exact hardware models during your pilot; hardware is quoted separately.

Yes.
