Questions

Good to know

What do I need to start?

An Android tablet for the kiosk (around ₹12,000) and any phone, tablet or computer for the counter and kitchen. A thermal printer and a TV for tokens are optional.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, the counter keeps billing offline. Bills are saved on the tablet and sync automatically when the connection returns. UPI on the kiosk needs internet.

Which payments are supported?

UPI through Razorpay or PhonePe with automatic confirmation, plus cash and card at the counter.

How do the Pro trial and billing work?

New cafes get 30 days of Pro, starting with their first real bill. Choose a monthly or yearly plan from the owner billing page; yearly billing includes two months free. You can also apply eligible promo codes or sign up with a referral code.

Can I take bulk and catering orders?

Yes, on Starter and Pro. Record the customer, where the order came from, the event, who is responsible and the chef. OrderNest shows what stock to buy, takes the ingredients from your normal stock when cooking starts, and tracks the advance and balance.

Can I manage my cafe from my laptop?

On Pro, yes: sign in from any web browser to manage the menu, stock, reports and settings. On Free and Starter, owner settings are in the OrderNest app, and the counter, kitchen and stock screens work in any browser.

Is it available in Tamil?

Yes. The kiosk menu, token screen and voice ordering work in Tamil and English.

Who owns my data?

You do. We don't show ads or sell data, and you can delete your cafe account at any time. See our privacy policy.
