Good to know

Starting a new cafe

I haven't opened yet. Can OrdenZ help me get ready?

Yes. Sign up free; the 30-day Pro trial only starts with your first real bill, so set-up time doesn't count. The Get your cafe ready checklist on the Owner screen walks you through the menu, bill details, payments, team, printer and kiosk. Ask us for practice mode and sample customers will order every few minutes so your team can train before opening day.

Which licences do I need to open a cafe?

In India, usually FSSAI registration or a state licence, a shop and establishment registration, a trade licence from your local body, and GST once you cross the threshold. In Malaysia, SSM registration, a premises and signboard licence from your local council, food handler training and typhoid vaccination for staff, and SST once you cross the threshold. Our starter guide explains each one, with links to the official sites.

Do I need to charge GST or SST from day one?

Not always. In India a restaurant generally registers for GST once its turnover passes ₹20 lakh a year (₹10 lakh in some states). In Malaysia, food and beverage businesses register for service tax at RM1.5 million a year. Until then, set the tax to 0% (India) or leave the SST number empty (Malaysia), and OrdenZ prints bills without tax. Check your case with an accountant.

What equipment should I budget for?

At minimum, one Android tablet or a laptop for the counter. Add a second tablet for the kiosk, a Bluetooth or network thermal printer (58 or 80 mm), a cash drawer that opens from the printer, and any TV with a browser for the token screen. Start with the counter and add the rest as you grow.

Using OrdenZ

What do I need to start?

A compatible Android tablet for the kiosk and a browser device for the counter or kitchen. A thermal printer and token display are optional. Test the exact hardware models during your pilot; hardware is quoted separately.

Can I try it before my first customer?

Yes. Try the live demo any time, or ask us to turn on practice mode for your own cafe: a sample customer orders about every 5 minutes for a week, so you can watch the kiosk, kitchen screen, token TV and reports working and train your team. Practice bills are marked and are removed when you tap Start selling for real, so your first real bill is bill 1.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, the counter keeps billing offline. Bills are saved on the tablet and sync automatically when the connection returns. UPI on the kiosk needs internet.

Which payments are supported?

In India, UPI through Razorpay or PhonePe with automatic confirmation, plus cash and card at the counter. In Malaysia, cash (rounded to 5 sen), card and manual recording of DuitNow payments at the counter; automatic DuitNow QR generation and payment confirmation are not currently included. Discuss the payment provider before going live.

Does OrdenZ work in Malaysia?

Yes. Choose Malaysia when you sign up: prices are in RM, the kiosk speaks English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese (Tamil too if you like), and bills show your SSM number. Add your SST registration number to charge 6% SST, and set a service charge if you have one; SST is then charged on the service charge too. Not SST-registered? Leave SST at 0%. We're starting with a few pilot cafes in Malaysia: ask us to join.

How do the Pro trial and billing work?

New cafes get 30 days of Pro, starting with their first real bill. India owners can use configured subscription checkout; Malaysia plans require a quote. Monthly and yearly totals are shown in the pricing section. You can also apply eligible promo codes or sign up with a referral code.

What is TableTurn?

TableTurn manages your tables and your team. Take bookings and keep a waiting list on Starter and Pro. On Pro, when guests leave a clean-up job goes to a staff member, a photo of the clean table makes it free for the next guests, and customers can give staff a star from the QR code on their bill.

Can I take bulk and catering orders?

Yes, on Starter and Pro. Record the customer, where the order came from, the event, who is responsible and the chef. OrdenZ shows what stock to buy, takes the ingredients from your normal stock when cooking starts, and tracks the advance and balance.

Can I manage my cafe from my laptop?

On Pro, yes: sign in from any web browser to manage the menu, stock, reports and settings. On Free and Starter, owner settings are in the OrdenZ app, and the counter, kitchen and stock screens work in any browser.

Which languages does it support?

The kiosk works in English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese; each cafe picks up to four. You can type your menu names in each language, or on Pro let AI fill them in for you to check. Voice input needs microphone permission and compatible device and service support.

Can I control what each staff member sees?

Yes. Give each person a role: manager, captain, chef, cashier, store keeper or cleaning. They sign in with their own PIN and see only the screens for their job. Managers can run the day but can't change your plan or billing. An activity log shows who changed what.

Can I keep track of supplier bills?

Yes, on Starter and Pro. Enter each supplier's bill with its items and GST, add a photo, and the stock and costs update by themselves. You see what you owe each supplier, what's overdue, the GST you paid this month, and your wastage.

What can I try in the demo?

Everything a cafe does in a day: order on the kiosk, cook it in the kitchen, split a bill at the counter, accept a table QR order, close the cash drawer, see the owner's reports, Tally export and all outlets, and the Malaysian cafe with SST and MyInvois files. Payments are simulated and nothing reaches real customers. See the demo page for a step-by-step tour.

Can I move from another POS?

You can enter menu items manually or use the Excel setup template. AI image import needs configuration and a review of names and prices. Ask us to assess your current export before switching; historical sales and supplier data are not automatically migrated.

Which connections need extra setup?

Indian automatic UPI confirmation needs an approved and configured payment provider. AI and WhatsApp need their own service credentials and may incur usage fees. Malaysian DuitNow recording is manual; OrdenZ prepares MyInvois e-invoice files, but your provider submits them, and delivery-app orders are entered at the counter (OrdenZ doesn't receive them from the apps directly). Confirm local tax requirements with your adviser.

What should I budget beyond the plan?

Allow for compatible hardware, payment-provider fees and any AI or messaging usage. Discuss setup, migration, training and ongoing support before purchasing. Malaysia subscriptions are quote-based; the displayed pilot prices do not enable MYR checkout.

Who owns my data?

You do. We don't show ads or sell data, and you can delete your cafe account at any time. See our privacy policy.
