OrdenZ live demo

A real cafe, open right now.

This is the real OrdenZ app with a sample cafe that never closes. Orders, table bookings, deliveries, clean-ups and staff stars arrive every few minutes. Be the owner, the cashier, the chef or the kiosk, and try anything: the demo cafe is reset every night.

The 10-minute tour

Things to try

Each one opens the right screen as the right person. Open two at once — the kiosk and the chef, or owner Recipes and chef Brief — to watch an order or a cook task travel.

1

Order on the kiosk

Tap Start, pick a latte, choose large with oat milk, and pay. The India demo uses a UPI code. The Malaysia demo uses DuitNow QR. After pay, you see the token and a QR to the English bill. Unpaid cash-at-counter is token only.

As the kiosk → 2

Cook it

Your order appears in a second. Tap Start, then Ready, and watch the number move to Ready. The TV says the number only when Speak the token is on in House.

As the chef → 3

Split a bill

Add three items, tap Split / more, choose Equal split, and pay one share by UPI and one in cash.

As the cashier → 4

Accept a table QR order

Guests order from the code on their table. On the table phone page, a customer enters the mobile number from a paid bill in the last 30 days and reorders that set. The server sets the price. Accept it at the top of the counter and it goes to the kitchen.

As the cashier → 5

Take a Swiggy order

Choose Delivery, then Swiggy. Type the order number from the cafe menu and send it to the kitchen. A signed webhook brings a live order only when that partner's webhook secret is set on the server. OrdenZ prices it from the cafe menu. If the secret is not set, the counter says "Automatic live orders are not connected."

As the cashier → 6

Close the cash drawer

Open Cash drawer. Shift sync shows the opening till, who is on, and the shift note. It is the till, not a bank card. Tap Count and close, type how many notes of each kind, and see the difference straight away.

As the cashier → 7

Enter a supplier bill

Open Intake, tap Enter an invoice, add a few items with their tax, and watch the stock and recipe costs change.

As the store keeper → 8

Check Money Review

On Owner: unusual money events for review, not a theft verdict, plus pickup slots. The WhatsApp digest is a preview only.

As the owner → 9

Prepare a MyInvois ZIP

Switch to the Kuala Lumpur cafe, open Reports, then e-Invoice: download the ZIP or submit through the mock provider. OrdenZ does not log in to MyInvois.

As the owner, Malaysia → 10

Open Brief

After PIN, Brief asks you to clock in — sign-in is not a clock. Skip to work, or Later / Can't / Done a cook task. Done does not cook a batch.

As the chef → 11

Captain clock-in

Same Brief screen after PIN. Clock in, or tap Skip to work to go to the floor. A cook task only shows if one was assigned to you.

As the captain → 12

Assign

On Recipes Cook soon (or Crew desk), tap Assign (or open Crew desk) to assign a Brief cook task. Marking it done does not cook a batch.

As the owner → 13

Seat table 4

As the captain, add items and tap Pay later. Pick a floor table. There are no cash, UPI or card buttons. The cashier later taps Pay T4.

As the captain → 14

Save a purchase draft

Open Intake → Drafts. The store keeper can draft from stock vs reorder. Approve needs the owner or manager.

As the store keeper → 15

Open pickup slots

On Owner, set the slot length and the cap. Share the public link. A takeaway stays unpaid until the customer collects it. The cashier records the payment then. Paying online for pickup stays off.

Choose a screen

Open the demo as…

Each role sees only what its job needs, just like in your cafe. The colours match the app's tabs.

Self-order kiosk

Photo menu and voice. The India demo pays with a UPI code. After pay, you see the token and a QR to the English bill. Unpaid cash-at-counter is token only.

Owner

Crew desk / Recipes Assign, then Owner for sales, Money Review, pickup slots, payouts, the day book and Tally import.

Cashier

Counter billing with Pay by table, split bills, pickup collection, delivery-app orders, the cash drawer, and Brief clock-in. After two idle minutes the till asks for this cashier's PIN.

Chef

Brief first: clock in and the cook task from Assign. Then tickets, recipes and batch cooking. Done does not cook a batch.

Captain

Brief to clock in (or Skip to work), then the floor: Pay later on a table, bookings, the waitlist QR and table QR orders.

Store keeper

Intake: supplier bills with tax and a photo, purchase drafts from stock vs reorder, wastage and what you owe each supplier.

Manager

Crew desk / Recipes Assign, then the day: Board, Crew desk PINs, clock-in hours, offers and reports. Not the plan or billing.

Cleaning

Their own clean-up jobs, finished with a photo of the clean table.

Token TV

Order numbers in the languages ticked in House, with a chime. Speak the token is off until you turn it on in House.
Malaysia

The Kuala Lumpur demo cafe

The same app set up for Malaysia: prices in RM, a 10% service charge on dine-in bills and 6% SST, cash rounded to 5 sen, DuitNow QR for the exact ringgit, and MyInvois files. Kiosk languages follow House. A new Malaysia cafe starts with English, Malay, Chinese, Tamil and Hindi.

Kiosk in Bahasa Melayu

Teh Tarik, Nasi Lemak and the coffee menu. Languages follow House: English, Malay, Chinese, Tamil and Hindi until you untick them.

Cashier (Malaysia)

DuitNow QR, a dine-in service charge, SST, and 5-sen rounding on cash. Card and DuitNow stay exact. SSM and SST numbers show on the bill.

Owner (Malaysia)

Sales in RM, SST collected, Intake, Bulk and a MyInvois ZIP via the cafe's provider — not an IRBM login.

Payments in the demo are simulated, and nothing reaches real customers. Passwords, the plan and WhatsApp sending are switched off.
