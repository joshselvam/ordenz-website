FAQ

Straight answers for Indian cafes

How does the Pro trial work?

Set up free. 30 days of Pro from the first real bill, or from day 15 if you have not billed yet. Then Free (1 paired device, 50 orders/day) unless you pick Starter or Pro. Practice bills keep the trial window open. Yearly is 12 months for the price of 10.

What are the India list prices?

Starter {{priceStarterIn}} / month and Pro {{priceProIn}} / month. These are software list prices from config.

Does offline counter work?

Offline counter billing is on Starter and Pro. Free needs a connection. UPI and table QR always need internet.

How does UPI work?

UPI QR needs a connected payment gateway in the app. Cash and card stay the exact amount on the bill.

When is GST charged on cafe bills?

GST appears on cafe bills after you add a GSTIN in House and set the rate (5% for most cafes). Until then OrdenZ prints plain bills.
