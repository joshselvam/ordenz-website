Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Applies to the OrderNest app and website

OrderNest is a self-order kiosk and point-of-sale (POS) app for cafes and restaurants, made by {{company}}. The app connects to the OrderNest server that the cafe uses. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and who sees it.

Who is responsible

Customers ordering at a kiosk don't need an account. UPI payments are made in the customer's own UPI app; OrderNest only receives the payment confirmation and reference from the cafe's payment gateway (Razorpay or PhonePe). OrderNest never sees bank or card details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • We don't sell or share data with data brokers.
  • No location tracking.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. Our host, Cloudflare, keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the site. The live demo runs only in your browser and keeps its sample data there.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Sign-in uses signed tokens, repeated sign-in attempts are slowed down, and devices such as kiosks can only do kiosk tasks. Each cafe can only see its own data.

Keeping and deleting data

Sales records are kept for as long as the cafe needs them for its accounts and tax records. A cafe owner can delete the cafe account and all its data at any time. See how to delete your account. Customers can ask the cafe to remove their loyalty number.

Children

OrderNest is a business app for cafe staff and customers placing food orders. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
