A day at your cafe

One app, from opening to closing

Here's a normal Saturday with OrdenZ. Each colour is a part of the app, the same colour you'll see on its tab.

  1. 7:00

    Clock in, open the drawer

    After PIN, Brief asks staff to clock in — sign-in is not a clock. Skip to work is allowed. The cashier counts ₹2,000 of float into the cash drawer.

    Brief · Cash drawer · Cashier
  2. 8:30

    The morning rush orders itself

    Office regulars order filter kaapi on the kiosk in Tamil or English and pay by UPI. The token TV shows their number when it's ready. It says the number only if Speak the token is on in House.

    Self-order kiosk · Token TV
  3. 9:00

    The kitchen sees everything at once

    Tickets from the kiosk, counter and tables arrive in a second. Orders waiting too long turn amber, then red.

    Kitchen screen · Chef
  4. 12:30

    Lunch at the tables

    The captain seats table 4 and taps Pay later — no cash buttons. Extra phones add lines to that same open check. The cashier settles with Pay T4.

    Captain · Group order · Pay by table
  5. 3:00

    The milk delivery arrives

    The store keeper saves a purchase draft from what's below reorder. The owner approves it, then the bill is entered and stock goes up.

    Intake · Drafts · Store keeper
  6. 4:00

    Pickup before dinner

    A guest books a 4:15 slot on the public link. The kitchen sees the cap. The ticket stays unpaid until the cashier collects at the counter.

    Pickup slots · Kitchen · Cashier
  7. 7:00

    Dinner on the delivery apps

    A Swiggy order lands from a signed webhook, or the cashier types it. Prices come from your cafe menu (with that app's mark-up) — not the partner app's listed prices. OrdenZ records that app commission and what the app still owes you.

    Delivery apps · Cashier
  8. 10:30

    Close, count, go home

    The drawer is counted note by note and the Z report prints. The owner checks the day's sales from home and sends the day book to the accountant.

    Reports · Owner

Keep your team focused on service

Runs on budget tablets

An Android tablet for the kiosk, any browser for the counter and kitchen, and a Bluetooth or network thermal printer if you want one. Test your exact models before you buy.

Your customers' language

India: English, Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu and Kannada. Malaysia: English, Malay, Chinese, Tamil and Hindi. Tick up to six in House. English stays available. Customers switch with one tap.

Fair, simple pricing

Free for one device and 50 orders a day. Starter and Pro when you grow, monthly or yearly with pricing confirmed before purchase. No card needed to start.

Offline billing on Starter and Pro

Wi-Fi down at lunch? On Starter and Pro the counter saves bills on the tablet and sends them, never twice, when you're back online. Receipts still print. Free needs a connection. UPI, DuitNow and table QR always need internet.

Your data stays yours

No ads, no selling data. Customers order without signing up. Download your sales any time, or delete your account and everything with it.
