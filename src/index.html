A practical first step

Bring your menu. Test your real workflow.

Use this checklist to plan a pilot before switching your cafe over.

01 · Prepare

Set up your cafe

Choose India or Malaysia, add your menu by Excel or manually, and review prices, taxes and language settings. AI photo import needs a configured provider and a review of the results.

02 · Practise

Try a full service

Run a sample order through the counter, kitchen and token display. Check your actual Android device, printer and connectivity before serving customers.

03 · Decide

Agree what you need

Compare the plan, payment setup and any hardware, messaging or AI costs. Ask about migration, training and support scope before committing.
