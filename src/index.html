A day at your cafe

One app, from opening to closing

Here's a normal Saturday with OrdenZ. Each colour is a part of the app, the same colour you'll see on its tab.

  1. 7:00

    Open the drawer

    The cashier counts ₹2,000 of float into the cash drawer. The shift starts, and every rupee from now on is accounted for.

    Cash drawer · Cashier
  2. 8:30

    The morning rush orders itself

    Office regulars order filter kaapi on the kiosk in Tamil or English and pay by UPI. The token TV calls their number when it's ready.

    Self-order kiosk · Token TV
  3. 9:00

    The kitchen sees everything at once

    Tickets from the kiosk, counter and tables arrive in a second. Orders waiting too long turn amber, then red.

    Kitchen screen · Chef
  4. 12:30

    Lunch at the tables

    A family orders from the QR code on table 4. Staff accept it, the kitchen cooks it, and the bill is split: half UPI, half cash, change worked out.

    Table QR · Split bills · Captain
  5. 3:00

    The milk delivery arrives

    The store keeper enters the supplier's bill with a photo. Stock goes up, recipe costs update, and the low-milk alert disappears.

    Suppliers · Stock · Store keeper
  6. 7:00

    Dinner on the delivery apps

    Swiggy and Zomato orders go in at their own app prices. OrdenZ keeps track of each app's commission and what it still owes you.

    Delivery apps · Cashier
  7. 10:30

    Close, count, go home

    The drawer is counted note by note and the Z report prints. The owner checks the day's sales from home and sends the day book to the accountant.

    Reports · Owner
