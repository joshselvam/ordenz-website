Delete your OrderNest account

Deleting your cafe account permanently removes your cafe, menu, orders, staff and loyalty records. This can't be undone.

In the app

  1. Open OrderNest and sign in as Owner.
  2. Go to Menu & settings, scroll to the bottom and open Cafe settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with your password. Your cafe and all its data are deleted straight away.
