FAQ

Straight answers for MY cafes

How does the Pro trial work?

Set up free. 30 days of Pro from the first real bill, or from day 15 if you have not billed yet. Then Free (1 paired device, 50 orders/day) unless you pick Starter or Pro. Practice bills do not start the trial. Yearly is 12 months for the price of 10.

What are the Malaysia list prices?

Starter {{priceStarterMy}} / month and Pro {{priceProMy}} / month. These are software list prices from config. Malaysia billing is settled by invoice.

Does offline counter work?

Offline counter billing is on Starter and Pro. Free needs a connection. DuitNow and table QR always need internet.

How does DuitNow work?

Automatic DuitNow QR needs an approved Fiuu Offline Payment merchant. Cash rounds to 5 sen; DuitNow and card stay the exact amount.

When is SST charged?

SST 6% only after the cafe adds an SST number. Leave it empty and OrdenZ prints bills without tax until then.

What about MyInvois?

Reports → e-Invoice prepares MyInvois files for your provider. That pack is a paid extra. OrdenZ does not log in to MyInvois for you.
