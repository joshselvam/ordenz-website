The 10-minute tour

Nine things to try

Each one opens the right screen as the right person. Open two at once, say the kiosk and the chef, to watch an order travel.

1

Order on the kiosk

Tap Start, pick a latte, choose large with oat milk, and pay with the UPI code. Try switching the language.

As the kiosk → 2

Cook it

Your order appears in a second. Tap Start, then Ready, and watch the token TV call the number.

As the chef → 3

Split a bill

Add three items, tap Split / more, choose Equal split, and pay one share by UPI and one in cash.

As the cashier → 4

Accept a table QR order

Guests order from the code on their table. Accept it at the top of the counter and it goes to the kitchen.

As the cashier → 5

Take a Swiggy order

Choose Delivery, then Swiggy: prices switch to the app's menu. Type the app's order number and send it to the kitchen.

As the cashier → 6

Close the cash drawer

Open Cash drawer, tap Count and close, type how many notes of each kind, and see the difference straight away.

As the cashier → 7

Enter a supplier bill

Tap Enter an invoice, add a few items with their tax, and watch the stock and recipe costs change.

As the store keeper → 8

Check the books

In Reports: what each delivery app still owes, the GST summary with a Tally export, and all outlets on one screen.

As the owner → 9

Prepare a MyInvois file

Switch to the Kuala Lumpur cafe, open Reports, then e-Invoice: the month's consolidated e-invoice is ready to download.

Choose a screen

Open the demo as…

Each role sees only what its job needs, just like in your cafe. The colours match the app's tabs.

Self-order kiosk

What customers see: a photo menu in four languages, voice ordering and a UPI code for the exact amount.

Owner

Today's sales, top items and busy hours, then Reports for delivery-app payouts, the Tally export and all your outlets.

Cashier

Counter billing with split bills, open tabs, delivery-app orders, table QR orders to accept, and the cash drawer.

Chef

Live tickets from every source, standard recipes, batch cooking and the catering orders to cook.

Captain

Every table at a glance, bookings, the waiting list, and table QR orders waiting for a yes.

Store keeper

Supplier bills with tax and a photo, stock counts, wastage and what you owe each supplier.

Manager

Runs the day: menu and prices, team PINs, offers and reports. Not the plan or the billing.

Cleaning

Their own clean-up jobs, finished with a photo of the clean table.

Token TV

Order numbers on any TV as they're ready, in two languages, with a chime.

Teh Tarik, Nasi Lemak and the coffee menu, in Malay, Chinese or English.

Owner (Malaysia)

Sales in RM, SST collected, suppliers, catering and MyInvois e-invoice files.
