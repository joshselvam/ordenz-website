OrdenZ guide

How to run your cafe on OrdenZ

Short, step-by-step help with pictures. Pick your role to see what matters to you, or search.

Board = menu & prices. Token screen = TV order numbers. Do not mix them up.

Try it in the live demo

1. Set up your cafe

Set up free. 30 days of Pro from the first real bill or from day 15 if you have not billed yet. Then Free (1 paired device, 50 orders a day) unless you pick Starter or Pro. Practice bills do not start the trial.

  1. Open OrdenZ, choose New cafe, and enter your cafe name, Country (India or Malaysia), your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters). Country sets currency, tax and kiosk languages. You can change it until the first real bill.
  2. You land on Owner. Follow Get your cafe ready in order: menu, address and phone on bills, then optional pay, team, printer and kiosk. Take the first real bill last.
  3. Under the checklist, Ask how to set this up points at the next unfinished step. Tap a question or Ask the assistant. It will not refund, send a campaign, or delete anything.
  4. Add your menu: type items, use the sample menu, upload the Excel template, or (Pro) photograph a printed menu and check the items before saving.
  5. In House fill in the details that print on bills: GSTIN, FSSAI and UPI.
  6. In House fill in the details that print on bills: SSM, SST if registered, and Fiuu for automatic DuitNow QR.

The longer walkthrough with pictures (sign-in through first bill, then tables, Tally, e-invoice, outlets and attendance) is the getting started guide.

Practice first, with sample customers

Want to see OrdenZ busy before your first real customer, or train your team? Ask us to turn on practice mode for your cafe. A sample customer then orders about every 5 minutes for a week, on the kiosk or at the counter, so you can watch the kitchen screen, token TV, stock and reports working. If your menu is still empty, we add a sample menu so there's something to order.

  • Practice bills print PRACTICE BILL · NOT A TAX INVOICE. They don't count for tax or the Free plan's daily limit, and they don't start your trial.
  • Orders you place yourself in practice mode are practice bills too, so try anything.
  • When you're ready, tap Start selling for real on the yellow banner. Every practice bill is removed, the stock goes back, and your first real bill is bill 1. You can remove the sample menu at the same time.

2. Your country: India or Malaysia

  1. When you sign up, choose your Country. It sets your currency, taxes, bill format, phone numbers and kiosk languages. You can change it in House until your first real bill.
  2. India: add your GSTIN, FSSAI number and GST rate. Menu prices include GST. Included GST is labelled as included and is not added on top of the total. Bills show CGST and SGST once your GSTIN is saved; until then they are plain bills with no GST.
  3. Malaysia: add your SSM registration no.. If you're SST-registered, add your SST registration no. and set SST rate to 6%; otherwise leave it at 0%.
  4. Choose Menu prices: Service charge + SST on top (set the Service charge %, usually 10%), or Nett (all included). Tap Save settings.
  5. Cash bills are rounded to the nearest 5 sen. Card and DuitNow QR stay the exact amount. Service charge is dine-in only. SST shows only after you save an SST number.

3. Connect the kiosk, TV and printer

Free is enough for one counter in a browser. Free includes 1 paired device. A paired kiosk, token TV, Showcase or Guest uses that slot. Kiosk plus a TV needs Starter (5 devices). A kitchen phone with a staff PIN is not a paired device. A TV does count.

  1. In House, find Kiosk and TV devices. It shows your cafe code and device key.
  2. On the kiosk tablet or TV, open OrdenZ, choose Kiosk / TV, enter the cafe code and device key, and pick Self-order kiosk, Token screen (TV), Showcase or Guest. Showcase and Guest are not their own tabs. Showcase shows one language. Edit slides on Offers → Ads & Showcase. Menu-list slides work without the Ads add-on. Today's special and Offer slides, the Token left strip, and Guest featured ads need the yearly Ads add-on (Plan → Add yearly), then House can turn Feature ads on Guest. A dish with a stored photo shows that photo. A dish with no photo shows the name and price. A sold-out dish is not the photo on a menu slide. Hidden or sold-out dishes drop off a special or a menu list. Promo codes stay at checkout. Kitchen wait shows as "About N min" once there are enough real ready tickets. It is an estimate, not the clock.
  3. The token screen, Showcase and Guest follow the app's light or dark theme.
  4. Tap Printer on the counter or kitchen screen to connect a Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or USB thermal printer (58 mm or 80 mm). Thermal slips use the English name. Picture receipts in Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada are not available yet. Stock, supplier, offer and kitchen documents print as compact 58/80 mm slips when a thermal printer is set. Failed jobs wait in the print queue.
  5. If a tablet is lost, tap Create a new device key. A new key signs every kiosk and TV out. Pair them again with the new key. Staff PIN sign-in then works only on devices you sign in to again as the owner.

4. Add your team and give everyone a role

Each person signs in with their own PIN and sees only the screens their job needs.

  1. Go to Crew desk (team, assign, hours) → Team.
  2. Type the name, a 4–6 digit PIN, choose the Role and tap Add team member.
  3. On the tablet, they choose Staff PIN and type their PIN. Staff PINs only work on the cafe's own devices — easiest is the owner signs in on that tablet once, or they enter the device key from House the first time.
  4. To change a role or a PIN, or stop someone signing in, tap Edit next to their name. It works within seconds, even on a tablet that's already signed in.

5. How customers order on the kiosk

  1. The customer taps the screen, chooses their language at the top, and picks Eat here or Take away.
  2. They tap items, choose size, milk and extras, or tap Order by voice (Pro) and just say it. "Would you like to add?" suggests a matching item.
  3. They pay with a UPI QR for the exact amount (Razorpay or PhonePe), or with cash or card at the counter. Payment confirms itself. Nobody has to check a phone.
  4. They pay with a DuitNow QR for the exact amount, or with cash or card at the counter. Payment confirms itself. Nobody has to check a phone.
  5. They see the token number. If the bill is paid, they also see a QR to the English bill on their phone. The caption above the QR follows the languages you ticked in House, for example "Scan for your bill". Cash at the counter stays token only until the bill is paid. If the kiosk shows a wait, it says "About N min" once there are enough real ready tickets. That is an estimate, not the clock.
  6. The TV always chimes when the order is ready. It says the number only when Speak the token is on in House. That switch is off until you turn it on. It does not speak on every cafe by default. When it is on, it uses the device's natural voice and says the token number only, in the language that guest chose on the kiosk (or the cafe's first language), such as "Token 1 4". It does not read the dish names. Guest does not speak.

9. The cash drawer

Know at the end of every shift that the cash adds up.

  1. At the start of the shift, open Cash drawer and count the float into it. Shift sync shows the opening till, who is on, and the shift note. It is the till, not a bank card.
  2. Took money out to pay a delivery, or added change from the bank? Tap Cash out or Cash in and give a reason.
  3. At closing, tap Count and close and type how many notes and coins of each kind you have. OrdenZ shows what should be there and the difference.
  4. A difference above your limit goes to the owner or a manager to review. Print the Z report for your records.

12. Tables, bookings and clean-ups (TableTurn) — Pro

TableTurn (bookings, waitlist QR, table QR and pickup slots) is on Pro. You can also Request it from Plan. Clean-up photos and staff stars stay on Pro.

  1. Open TableTurn to see every table: free, reserved, seated or needs cleaning. Staff can move an open check to another table, or merge two open checks when guests join.
  2. On Pro, tap New booking for a reservation; OrdenZ suggests the right-size table and never double-books. Walk-ins go on Add to waiting list.
  3. Print the Waiting list QR and put it at the door. Guests scan, join and see the wait in minutes. No app.
  4. On Pro, when guests leave, tap the table. The clean-up job goes to the cleaning staff automatically.

  6. On Pro, customers can give staff a star from the QR code on their bill.

14. Stock, recipes (Pro) and wastage

  1. Every sale takes its ingredients out of stock using the item's recipe. Low items show an alert; sold-out items are marked and cannot be added on the kiosk or the counter. Hiding an item is a separate Board switch.
  2. In Recipes, write a batch recipe once and tap Cook batch; the raw ingredients come off stock. Owner or manager: Assign on Cook soon assigns a Brief cook task. Done on that task does not cook a batch. Tap Prep report for a kitchen sheet. With a thermal printer set, it prints as a compact 58/80 mm slip.
  3. On Stock, tap Count / edit after a stock count.
  4. Thrown something away? Tap Wastage, enter how much and why. Its cost shows on the Intake page.
